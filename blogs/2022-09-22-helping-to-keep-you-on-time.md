Human perception is incredibly sensitive to disjointed rhythms, so for musicians, timing is everything. And they’ve been using metronomes for a couple hundred years to keep themselves in sync. Now metronomes have become a truly essential tool for artists working independently on recording projects, allowing them to lock-in to the same tempo when different parts are being captured across different sessions.
